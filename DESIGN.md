# Honeycomb Harmonic design direction

User direction, October 20, 2026: use Apple as the primary design reference while keeping the darker color format. Every image should be elegant and photorealistic, with high production quality.

## Interface

The reference is Apple's current website: https://www.apple.com/ and its product presentation at https://www.apple.com/iphone/.

Use its discipline of compact navigation, clear sans-serif hierarchy, generous spacing, singular product focus, and simple primary/secondary actions. Maintain Honeycomb Harmonic's original identity: near-black and charcoal surfaces, warm ivory type, restrained gold, and a muted burgundy practice section.

Keep all routes coherent, including the book reader, journal, contact page, and small-screen menu. Prefer semantic HTML and progressive enhancement. Product status is honest and legible.

## Imagery

The book, balm, and holder use distinct photorealistic studio visualizations with coherent low-key lighting and dark backgrounds. They are labeled as developmental or conceptual work. They are not proof of a manufactured product, an approved final cover, or validated fit.

The actual session photographs remain authentic. Only responsive resizing and compression are applied to their files. Monochrome presentation is a reversible CSS treatment. Generative retouch candidates were not used because they changed person/scene details.

The logo and frozen cap master are preserved as source authority. Generated product pictures are illustrative applications, never replacement artwork masters or dimension sources. Following the user's rejection of the split emblem and request to redesign the physical object, the complete front flower and all three nested outlines belong entirely on the glass jar body, clear of the shoulder and base. The cap sidewall stays plain black; the approved circular top retains its artwork. No emblem or botanical line crosses the closure, and the composition does not depend on the screw cap returning to a particular orientation. Keep cap and glass edges black without a white or silver perimeter ring. The current redesign uses `balm-studio-v3-*`; earlier seam-spanning and split-emblem pictures are rejected for current website use.

No tongue depressor, wooden applicator stick, renamed spatula, or equivalent prop may appear in any current or future image or demonstration.
