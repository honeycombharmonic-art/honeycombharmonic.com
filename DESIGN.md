# Honeycomb Harmonic design direction

User direction, October 20, 2026: use Apple as the primary design reference while keeping the darker color format. Every image should be elegant and photorealistic, with high production quality.

## Interface

The reference is Apple's current website: https://www.apple.com/ and its product presentation at https://www.apple.com/iphone/.

Use its discipline of compact navigation, clear sans-serif hierarchy, generous spacing, singular product focus, and simple primary/secondary actions. The user's subsequent color correction calls for a rich dark palette: forest green, burgundy, copper, teal, warm ivory, and gold. Dark must not mean colorless.

Keep all routes coherent, including the book reader, journal, contact page, and small-screen menu. Prefer semantic HTML and progressive enhancement. Product status is honest and legible.

## Imagery

The book, balm, and holder use distinct photorealistic studio visualizations with coherent low-key lighting and dark backgrounds. They are labeled as developmental or conceptual work. They are not proof of a manufactured product, an approved final cover, or validated fit.

The actual session photographs remain authentic and in color. Only responsive resizing and compression are applied to their files. Generative retouch candidates were not used because they changed person/scene details.

The logo and frozen cap master are preserved as source authority. Generated product pictures are illustrative applications, never replacement artwork masters or dimension sources. Following the user's rejection of the split emblem and request to redesign the physical object, the complete front flower and all three nested outlines belong entirely on the glass jar body, clear of the shoulder and base. The cap sidewall stays plain black; the approved circular top retains its artwork. No emblem or botanical line crosses the closure, and the composition does not depend on the screw cap returning to a particular orientation. Keep cap and glass edges black without a white or silver perimeter ring. The current redesign uses `balm-studio-v5-*`; earlier seam-spanning and split-emblem pictures are rejected for current website use.

No tongue depressor, wooden applicator stick, renamed spatula, or equivalent prop may appear in any current or future image or demonstration.

October 20 photographic refinement: keep decoration thin and flush to its substrate. UV glass appears black, never purple. Distinguish glass from molded plastic through realistic neutral reflections, not violet coloration. Use champagne gold and ivory and a quiet photographic backdrop. Avoid glowing lines, raised petal sculpture, artificial edge trim, and exaggerated procedural texture. Keep the body-only composition unchanged.

## Book identity and photographic review

October 20 user correction: the book artwork is the almost-touching fingers of Adam and God, within the honeycomb trio, with no flower. Follow the user's annotated rising diagonal: Adam reaches from lower left and God from upper right. Unify the hands, three outlines, title, and author name as one restrained gold foil treatment on black linen. The gap remains visible at the center. A brown painting pasted into a gold frame is superseded.

The hand-pose reference is Michelangelo's *The Creation of Adam*, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hands_of_God_and_Adam.jpg). This is a generated cover interpretation, not a reproduction master or an approved print production file.

The current cover combines a strong uppercase Roman-style title, anatomically referenced gold hands, upright nested hexagons, and the author name ANSON KEMPER in capitals. Typography references reviewed include Folio Society's The Prophet and Penguin's clothbound classics. The cover, detail photograph, and campaign share this identity.
