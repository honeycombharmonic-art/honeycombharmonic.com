The working brief

What the hands
ask of a balm.

  1. 01

    Pickup & melt

    Take a measured amount. Let it soften under the hands. The first seconds matter as much as the glide.

  2. 02

    Glide & control

    Enough slip for movement; enough purchase for focused work. That balance is the center of the formulation brief.

  3. 03

    The finish

    The work continues after the last stroke. Residue, skin feel, and the practitioner’s hands all belong in the brief.

The material direction

Three forms.
One shea foundation.

Firm shea. Soft Nilotica. Liquid shea.

West African shea, East African Nilotica, and fractionated liquid shea form the shared identity of the line. The jar balm and a separately developed pumpable format each have a non-cannabinoid and a CBD-distillate development track. Aroma is a separate choice.

Where the balm stands today

Formulation, stability, skin tolerability, packaging compatibility, and finished-product testing are still in development. Ingredient directions and proposed prices are available in the store. The final formula and release date are not yet set.

About the vessel

The selected vessel is the MIRON Sirius 200 mL jar. The vessel shown here is a photorealistic design visualization. It does not depict a manufactured retail product. Final decoration and packaging remain in development.
