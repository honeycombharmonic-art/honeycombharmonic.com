Harmonic Touch · The balm

Made around
the feel.

In development

A waterless professional bodywork balm built around West African shea, East African Nilotica shea, and fractionated liquid shea.

The development brief is simple: a medium that responds to the hands, with attention to every stage of contact.

The working brief

From first contact
to the finish.

  1. 01

    Pickup & melt

    The amount taken from the jar and the way it softens under the hands.

  2. 02

    Glide & control

    Movement when it is needed. Purchase for precise, deliberate work.

  3. 03

    The finish

    Attention to what remains on the skin and the practitioner’s hands.

The material direction

Three forms.
One shea foundation.

Firm shea. Soft Nilotica. Liquid shea.

These form the shared identity of the line. The jar balm and a separately developed pumpable format each have a non-cannabinoid and a CBD-distillate development track. Aroma is a separate choice.

Where the balm stands today

Formulation, stability, skin tolerability, packaging compatibility, and finished-product testing are still in development. Final ingredients, pricing, and a release date are not yet announced.

About the vessel

The selected vessel is the MIRON Sirius 200 mL jar. The cap artwork shown here is a design study, not a finished retail product photograph. Final decoration and packaging remain in development.
