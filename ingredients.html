Harmonic Touch · Materials

Inside Harmonic Touch.

Three forms of shea are the starting point. The supporting ingredients change with the job.

These are ingredients under development, not final formulas or labels. Exact grades and percentages are still being evaluated.

01

The three-shea foundation

West African shea, East African Nilotica, and fractionated liquid shea establish the shared material identity. Balm and pumpable use different ratios.

  • West African shea — structure
  • Nilotica shea — softer whole shea
  • Liquid shea — fluid carrier

02

Supporting oils

Jojoba, squalane, green tea seed oil, and bamboo extract form the supporting base. Each material’s grade and use level are still being evaluated.

  • Golden jojoba oil
  • Olive-derived squalane
  • Green tea seed oil
  • Oil-soluble bamboo extract in sunflower oil

03

Balm and pumpable

The balm uses beeswax for structure and arrowroot for texture. The first pumpable trials leave both out and increase the proportion of liquid shea.

  • Balm: beeswax and arrowroot
  • Pumpable: independent ratios and dispensing validation

04

The CBD expression

The CBD formulas have their own carrier and supporting ingredients. They are not part of the non-CBD base. CBD content will be established through formula development and lot testing. No separately added CBG is planned.

  • CBD distillate
  • C8 MCT carrier
  • Nerolidol
  • Alpha-bisabolol
  • Copaiba
  • Oil-compatible ceramide system

05

Practitioner’s Reserve · Grip

A localized additive concept that retains the three-shea identity. Rosin can cause contact sensitivity; its grade, concentration, process, and finished safety are still unresolved.

  • Three-shea foundation
  • Castor oil
  • Pure pine gum rosin / Colophonium
  • A modest increase in beeswax

06

Aroma and research

Aroma is a separate choice from format or CBD. The aromatic blend is still being selected. Hemisqualane, avocado oil, tallow, and emu oil remain research possibilities; they are not current core ingredients.

  • No final fragrance formula announced
  • Final ingredient and cannabinoid information will accompany each released formula

The formulation brief

The material
has to earn its place.

The formulation brief calls for natural materials and no synthetics.

Origin alone is not enough. Processing, consistency, compatibility, and the way a material behaves under the hands all need to be considered.
