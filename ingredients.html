Harmonic Touch · Materials

Every ingredient
has a place.

A clear view of the material direction—and what belongs in each expression.

These are development ingredients and intended roles. They are not released formulas, finished-product claims, or final label lists. Exact percentages and final grades remain in development.

01

The three-shea foundation

West African shea, East African Nilotica, and fractionated liquid shea establish the shared material identity. The ratios change with the format.

  • West African shea — structure
  • Nilotica shea — softer whole shea
  • Liquid shea — fluid carrier

02

Supporting oils

The supporting base direction is shared across the permanent formats. Ingredient grade, origin, processing, and use level remain to be qualified.

  • Golden jojoba oil
  • Olive-derived squalane
  • Green tea seed oil
  • Oil-soluble bamboo extract in sunflower oil

03

Balm and pumpable

The jar balm uses a minimum-functional-wax direction with arrowroot for texture. Initial pumpable trials omit beeswax and arrowroot, with liquid shea leading the carrier phase.

  • Balm: beeswax and arrowroot
  • Pumpable: independent ratios and dispensing validation

04

The CBD expression

These materials belong to the CBD development tracks, not the non-cannabinoid bases. No separately added CBG is planned. Final cannabinoid content will depend on the qualified distillate and testing.

  • CBD distillate
  • C8 MCT carrier
  • Nerolidol
  • Alpha-bisabolol
  • Copaiba
  • Oil-compatible ceramide system

05

Practitioner’s Reserve · Grip

A localized additive concept that retains the three-shea identity. Rosin can cause contact sensitivity; its grade, concentration, process, and finished safety are still unresolved.

  • Three-shea foundation
  • Castor oil
  • Pure pine gum rosin / Colophonium
  • A modest increase in beeswax

06

Aroma and research

Neutral and optional aroma are separate choices. The final aromatic composition is not elected. Hemisqualane, avocado oil, tallow, and emu oil remain controlled candidates or possible experiments, not automatic core ingredients.

  • No final fragrance formula announced
  • No blanket hypoallergenic, THC-free, or therapeutic claims

The formulation brief

Natural materials.
Specific decisions.

The direction is natural materials and zero synthetics.

That is a formulation constraint. Each purchased grade still needs qualification; a plant-derived name alone does not establish its processing or suitability.
