Privacy

A small site with a simple contact path.

This page explains what information the site asks for and what happens when you choose to email.

Information you choose to send

This site has no contact form, account, checkout, or newsletter signup. If you use an email link, the information you place in that message is sent through your email provider to Honeycomb Harmonic's email provider.

Keep sensitive details out of email

Please do not send medical records, diagnoses, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in an initial email.

Basic hosting data

Like most websites, the hosting service may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as an IP address, browser information, requested pages, and timestamps. Honeycomb Harmonic does not use this page to promise practices controlled by a hosting provider.

Questions

For a privacy question, email honeycombharmonic@gmail.com.
