Ask about a session

The approach

Depth without theatrics.

Sessions may include sustained contact, pressure, breath, movement, stillness, and simple practices to explore afterward. The exact approach is shaped together.

The aim is not to overpower the body or promise an outcome. It is to offer clear information and room for response.

What to expect

  • Before: a conversation about your goals, preferences, and relevant boundaries.
  • During: ongoing consent, adjustable pressure, and permission to pause or change course.
  • After: time to notice what changed and discuss any useful next steps.

Scope and boundaries

Bodywork is not medical diagnosis or treatment. It may complement care, but it does not replace a qualified healthcare professional. You remain in charge of what happens in a session.
