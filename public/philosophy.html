Philosophy

Touch is information.

The work begins with a simple premise: change is easier to explore when contact is believable, paced, and clear.

Nothing is forced.

What is held is heard. What is heard can change.

Working principles

  • Pressure is a conversation.

    Contact asks, listens, adjusts, and makes room for a clear no.

  • Atmosphere supports attention.

    Calm is useful when it helps a person orient, communicate, and stay present.

  • Education should become usable.

    Learning matters when it can be carried into ordinary movement, rest, touch, or practice.
