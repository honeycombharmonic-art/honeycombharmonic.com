Harmonic Touch · The articulated holder

See how it moves.

From the clamp to the working angle. A closer look at the holder’s intended operation.

46-second prototype film · Full HD
Read the film description
  1. Within reach. The black holder is positioned on the long side of the table near the head.
  2. Secure the clamp. The knob turns and the padded jaws close around the rail.
  3. Set the angle. The cam lever releases, the articulated joint moves, and the lever locks again.
  4. Lift. Access. Replace. The separate lid lifts vertically to reveal the balm, then returns to the cradle. The operator is omitted from this animation.
  5. Release the holder. The clamp loosens and lowers clear of the rail before the assembly lifts away.
  6. Change position. The holder moves along the outside of the table.
  7. Secure near the foot. The clamp closes in the new position on the long side.
  8. Ready for the next position. A final view shows the holder beside the full table.
