The structure

One inquiry.
Two volumes.

What is the pattern of right relation?

The philosophical foundations and symbolic history have their own volume. Bodywork, healing, relational work, and the building of societies have theirs.

Volume I · 10-chapter working structure

The Hidden Pattern

The philosophical underpinning: the Word, the forms that carry life, and the symbolic histories through which people have understood them.

  • The Word, divine naming, and the limits of images
  • The Two Cups: wine, communion, and sacred inversion
  • Arthur, Excalibur, the cauldron, and the Grail
  • Egypt and the relationship between symbol and order

The pattern, approached through philosophy and history.

Volume II · 23-chapter working structure

The Living Practice

The practical work: attending to the body, creating the container, and shaping relationships and environments in which human capacities can develop.

  • Bodywork, bodily communication, and healing
  • Fascination, attention, consent, and the container
  • Recognition, intimacy, and relational consequence
  • Community, society, and living social architecture

The pattern, embodied in how we live and work.

Working titles and chapter structure. Both manuscripts are in development; publication details are still to come.
