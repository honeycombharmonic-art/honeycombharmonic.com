Development notes

A different format.
The same attention to touch.

The pumpable retains all three sheas, with liquid shea taking the lead. The first trials omit the beeswax and arrowroot used in the jar balm.

The bottle is a packaging concept. Pump choice, fill quantity, dose per press, and performance across temperatures are still being tested.

Ingredient direction

Development materials, not a final INCI label or an ingredient list in concentration order.

  • West African shea
  • East African Nilotica shea
  • Fractionated liquid shea
  • Golden jojoba oil
  • Olive-derived squalane
  • Green tea seed oil
  • Oil-soluble bamboo extract in sunflower oil
